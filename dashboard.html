📖 How Corsair Unbound Works

Quick operating guide & setup instructions
⚡ 1. Arming Fortress

Open the extension popup on any site and click Add & Arm Fortress. All third-party popups and script-driven redirects on that site are terminated at the browser kernel via DNR session rules.

🌐 2. Smart Whitelist

500+ trusted domains built-in and synced from Cisco OpenDNS weekly. Whitelisted sites are never sent to VirusTotal — saving your API quota.

🔍 3. Auto Threat Scan

When you open a non-whitelisted site, Corsair queries VirusTotal and shows a banner in the top-right corner of the page.

🛡️ 4. DNR Catch-All

Every Fortress-armed profile installs a domainType: thirdParty session rule that blocks any main_frame navigation leaving the origin.

🧠 5. Heuristic Analysis

Corsair analyzes domains for typosquatting, suspicious TLDs, homograph attacks, and recently registered domains.

🎨 6. Custom Scripts

Add your own JS snippets per domain. They inject automatically every time you visit — perfect for removing annoying page overlays or tweaking styles.
