📖 How Corsair Unbound Works

Quick operating guide & setup instructions
⚡ 1. Arming Fortress

Open the extension popup on any site and click Add & Arm Fortress.

🌐 2. Smart Whitelist

500+ trusted domains built-in and synced from Cisco OpenDNS weekly.

🔍 3. Auto Threat Scan

When you open a non-whitelisted site, Corsair queries VirusTotal and shows a banner.

🛡️ 4. DNR Catch-All

Every Fortress-armed profile installs a domainType: thirdParty session rule.

🧠 5. Heuristic Analysis

Corsair analyzes domains for typosquatting, suspicious TLDs, homograph attacks.

🎨 6. Custom Scripts

Add your own JS snippets per domain.

⚡ 7. Auto-Arm Fortress

Sites whose threat score exceeds the Arm threshold are automatically Fortress-armed.

🚫 8. Manual Blocklist

Click 🚫 Block on any verdict banner to block a domain everywhere.
